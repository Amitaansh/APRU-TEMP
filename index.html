The theme

Bridging Resilience(s)

Amid climate change, economic and technological uncertainty, and social and environmental disruption, cities across the Pacific Rim face unprecedented challenges — yet the knowledge and practices of sustainable planning, governance and design remain siloed. The 2027 conference explores how they connect, and how bridging itself becomes a form of resilience.
